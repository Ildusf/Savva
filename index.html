Our story

A quiet corner of Madinah, built around good coffee.

Savva started with a simple idea: a place in Bir Uthman where the coffee is taken seriously and everything else is taken slow. No rush at the counter, no noise in the room — just carefully sourced beans, a barista who remembers your order, and light that changes with the day.

We brew espresso, V60 and drip; we whisk matcha by hand; we blend hibiscus into slush for the hot afternoons. And we bake — Madini cookies, cinnamon danish, pecan cake — every morning before the doors open at 6:30.
